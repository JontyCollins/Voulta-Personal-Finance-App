What Vault is

Vault is a personal finance tool designed to help you track and understand your finances. Think of it as a private notebook for your money — a calculator with memory. It runs in your browser, keeps your data private, and tries to help you see the bigger picture.

What Vault is not

Vault is not a regulated financial product. We are not authorised by the FCA or any other regulator. We don't hold your money, we don't move money for you, and we don't offer investment services.

Nothing in Vault — not the calculators, the projections, the tax estimates, the FIRE planner, or any other feature — constitutes financial advice, tax advice, or legal advice. It's information and tooling, not guidance. If you need advice, please speak to a qualified, regulated adviser.

You are solely responsible for any financial decisions you make using information from Vault. We do our best to make the calculations accurate, but tax rules change, market data can be stale, and you know your circumstances better than any calculator does. Sanity-check anything important against an authoritative source before acting on it.
